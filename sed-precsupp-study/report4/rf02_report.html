Droplet sedimentation, precipitation suppression and resolution in the Nd–LWP response

Prepared by Claude Code (AI assistant) on behalf of Naser Mahfouz, for Naser Mahfouz and Johannes Mülmenstädt · 10 November 2026

340 runs analysed 40 km → 312 m Nd 20–1000 cm⁻³ 12 h, hours 10–12 means 312 m: 76 % of model-hours
Abstract We ran DP-EAMxx on the DYCOMS-II RF02 nocturnal drizzling stratocumulus for 12 h. The matrix covers grid spacing from 40 km to 312 m, droplet number Nd from 20 to 1000 cm⁻³, a cloud-droplet sedimentation multiplier (×0.5, 1, 2) and precipitation suppression (KK2000 Nd dependence on or off). Below 1 km it also covers 3D turbulence on or off. At ≥10 km, LWP rises monotonically with Nd, and precipitation suppression explains all of that rise. As Δx shrinks, entrainment increases and depends more strongly on Nd. The suppression gain collapses, from +134 g m⁻² at 40 km to +6 g m⁻² at 312 m, and the LWP maximum moves to lower Nd. The result is an inverted V whose falling branch comes from entrainment: it persists with suppression off, and faster sedimentation strengthens it. Process-level diagnostics show the LWP cell scale converging near 17–20 km while LWP does not. Over the same range the turbulence shifts from parameterised to mostly resolved, and the rain budget nearly vanishes at fine resolution.

Key results

  • The inverted V's peak moves to lower Nd as Δx shrinks. With sed ×1 and suppression on, LWP peaks at Nd ≥ 1000 at ≥ 10 km, 500 at 5 km, 200 from 2.5 km to 625 m, and 100 at 312 m. The slope from Nd 100 to 200 falls monotonically: 0.27 at 40 km, 0.11 at 2.5 km, 0.03 at 625 m and −0.05 at 312 m.
  • Precipitation suppression fades with resolution. At Nd 1000, LWP with suppression on minus off is +134 g m⁻² at 40 km, +58 at 5 km, +34 at 2.5 km, +16 at 625 m and +6 at 312 m.
  • The falling branch comes from entrainment. At 312 m the slope from Nd 200 to 1000 is negative with suppression on (−0.02 / −0.04 / −0.07 for sed ×0.5 / 1 / 2) and also with it off. Meanwhile we keeps rising with Nd.
  • The low-Nd branch is robust; LWP is not converged. Below 5 km the slope from Nd 20 to 50 is about 0.6 / 0.7 / 0.9–1.0 for sed ×0.5 / 1 / 2 at every Δx. Between 625 m and 312 m at Nd 100, LWP drops from 120 to 76 g m⁻² and cloud cover from 0.92 to 0.83.
  • The cell scale converges. The dominant LWP wavelength approaches 17–20 km (aspect ratio ≈ 17) at 625–312 m.
  • The grey zone. The resolved share of boundary-layer w-variance is 0 at ≥ 10 km, 9 % at 2.5 km, 37 % at 625 m and 66 % at 312 m.
  • Rain nearly vanishes at fine resolution. At Nd 200, rain production falls from 0.5 to 0.03 mm d⁻¹ between 40 km and 312 m, and up to 93 % of it evaporates. That is why suppression stops mattering.
  • Entrainment per unit radiative driving roughly doubles. At saturated LW cooling (≈ 86 W m⁻²), we is 4.0, 6.1 and 7.8 mm s⁻¹ at 40 km, 2.5 km and 625 m.
  • 3D turbulence is negligible at 625 m and 312 m. |ΔLWP| is under 1.5 g m⁻², with we lower by less than 1 %.

Pathways

Each pathway is isolated by differencing runs that differ in one switch only. The suppression term crosses zero near Nd 63 by construction, since the autoconversion rates match there. Above Nd 100 it falls off steeply with Δx. The sedimentation term changes sign between the single-column regime and the km-scale regime.

Resolution dependence and cloud morphology

Process-level diagnostics

These come from the 15-min domain-mean profiles (hours 10–12), the 3D snapshots at hours 10 and 12 (resolved w′ and θv′, with w = −ω/ρg) and the 15-min column fields. All runs have 3D turbulence off. Decoupling follows Jones et al. (2011): the qt and θl differences between the lowest and highest quarter of the boundary layer. The cell scale is the peak of the radially averaged LWP power spectrum.

Cell scale converges; LWP does not

The dominant LWP wavelength falls from 30 km at 2.5 km to 24, 21 and 17 km at 1.25 km, 625 m and 312 m. In grid units it rises from 8 to about 37 physics cells, so below 1 km the cells are no longer grid-locked. The aspect ratio λ/zi approaches 17–20, a typical value for observed mesoscale cells. At ≥ 5 km the domain sets the scale (48 km = 240 km / 5).

The turbulence grey zone

At ≥ 10 km all turbulence is SHOC's, with a boundary-layer w-variance of 0.33–0.41 m² s⁻². Below 5 km SHOC's variance drops to about 0.09 m² s⁻² and keeps falling, while the resolved share grows: 6–9 % at 5–2.5 km, 17 % at 1.25 km, 37 % at 625 m and 66 % at 312 m. Total variance at km scale is about 4× smaller than in the single-column runs, yet entrainment is higher.

Stratocumulus-like or cumulus-like turbulence

At km scale, low Nd gives positive resolved w skewness, up to 1–2: narrow, strong updrafts in a cumulus-like, drizzle-decoupled boundary layer. High Nd gives the negative skewness of a radiatively driven Sc layer, −0.6 to −0.95 at 2.5 km–625 m. The switch happens near Nd 50–100 and moves to higher Nd with more sedimentation. At 312 m the skewness stays positive at every Nd (0.1–0.3 for Nd ≥ 100). That is a structural change still under way, and a reason to look at 156 m.

Drizzle-induced decoupling

At ≤ 5 km, Nd 20 has Δqt ≈ 1.1–1.6 g kg⁻¹ and Δθl ≈ 1.4–2.2 K, which is strongly decoupled. Both fall steeply with Nd, to 0.2–0.6 g kg⁻¹ and 0–0.7 K by Nd 1000. The single-column runs instead have a deep, SHOC-mixed cloud layer starting near 240 m. That is a structurally different boundary layer, not a coarse version of the km-scale one.

Why precipitation suppression fades with resolution

At Nd 200, column rain production (autoconversion plus accretion) falls from 0.5 mm d⁻¹ at 40 km to 0.03 at 312 m. The cloud layer thins from 600 m to 330 m and its base rises from 240 m to 680 m. The share of rain that evaporates before reaching the surface rises from about 0.4 to 0.93, and above Nd 100 the raining fraction is zero at ≤ 1.25 km. There is almost no rain left to suppress.

Mean structure

As Δx shrinks, the boundary layer deepens (zi 860 m at 40 km to 1010 m at 312 m), and the cloud layer thins and lifts. LW cooling at cloud top is sharper and stronger in the single-column runs.

Radiative driving and entrainment efficiency

Boundary-layer LW cooling saturates at about 86 W m⁻² for LWP above 120 g m⁻², so most runs share nearly the same radiative forcing. At that forcing the median we is 4.0 (40 km), 5.1 (5 km), 6.1 (2.5 km), 6.6 (1.25 km) and 7.8 mm s⁻¹ (625 m). Entrainment per unit radiative driving roughly doubles from the single-column limit to 625 m.

Diagnostic bug in P3

P3's sedimentation tendency outputs qc_sed, qr_sed and qi_sed cannot be used. The C++ sedimentation routines compute q_tend = (q − q_tend)/dt, which expects the buffer to hold the pre-sedimentation q. p3_main never loads it, whereas the Fortran sets qc_sed = qc first. The outputs therefore come out as about q/Δt; for example, qc_sed/qc = 1/(60 s) at 1.25 km. Model state is not affected, and the fix is three lines. This also explains the odd qc_sed values seen in the earlier ACI study.

3D turbulence

At 625 m (36 pairs), |ΔLWP| is under 1.5 g m⁻² and |Δcloud cover| under 0.007. At 312 m (16 pairs so far, Nd 20–200), |ΔLWP| ≤ 1.0 g m⁻² and |Δcloud cover| ≤ 0.006. The one consistent signal is we, which is 0.02–0.07 mm s⁻¹ lower (under 1 %).

Transience

At ≤ 2.5 km, LWP changes by at most 4.5 g m⁻² h⁻¹ over hours 10–12. At 40–10 km it is still rising at high Nd with suppression on, by up to +18 g m⁻² h⁻¹.

Setup

Case. DYCOMS-II RF02 nocturnal drizzling stratocumulus: shortwave off; T and q nudged above 650 hPa; IOP surface fluxes and subsidence. The forcing is constant over 0–12 h. We run doubly periodic EAMxx (SHOC, P3, SPA, RRTMGP; 128 levels).

Domains and steps. The domain is 600 km at 40 km, 300 km at 20 km, and 240 km from 10 km down. The physics step is 100 s at ≥ 5 km and 75, 60, 30 and 15 s at 2.5, 1.25, 0.625 and 0.3125 km.

Factors.

  • Δx: 8 levels, 40 km to 312 m.
  • Nd target: 20 to 1000 cm⁻³. The diagnosed Nd is 10–25 % above target at ≥ 625 m and 15–20 % below at 312 m.
  • p3::cloud_sedimentation_factor: 0.5, 1, 2. This is a new P3 option that scales the cloud-droplet fall speeds, like MG2's cld_sed.
  • Precipitation suppression: on, or off (KK2000 Nd exponent 0, prefactor 0.812, so the rates match at Nd 63).
  • 3D turbulence: on or off, at 625 m and 312 m only.

Diagnostics. Hour 10–12 means. Cloud cover is the fraction of columns with LWP above 10 g m⁻². we = dzi/dt − wsubs(zi). The sub-km runs ran in restart segments, which we checked are bit-for-bit identical to straight runs.

Status and data

312 m is 76 % done (658 of 864 model-hours). Every run with 3D turbulence off is complete; 20 of the 36 runs with it on remain. Perlmutter maintenance runs from 11 to 18 November, and any unfinished runs resume afterwards from their last restart. Open questions are 156 m and seed ensembles for the small negative susceptibilities.
