\noindent{\Large\bfseries Cloud sedimentation $\times$ precip suppression: full matrix, 40\,km to 1.25\,km}\\[2pt]
6 Nov 2026. Prepared by Claude Code (AI assistant) on behalf of Naser Mahfouz.
DP-EAMxx DYCOMS-II RF02, 6\,h; all 144 runs, single realizations; hour 4--6 means unless noted. Sub-km runs are on hold.

\section*{Findings}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{At $\geq$5\,km, the positive LWP--Nd response is all precip suppression.} With suppression off, LWP is flat at
170--175\,g\,m$^{-2}$ ($S\approx0$) wherever $w_e\approx4.9$\,mm\,s$^{-1}$.
\item \textbf{At coarse resolution, sedimentation switches entrainment between two levels.} At low Nd with sed$\times$1 or $\times$2,
$w_e$ drops to 2.5--2.9\,mm\,s$^{-1}$, with suppression on or off. With suppression off, this gives a negative low-Nd branch
($S_{\rm low}$ from $-0.3$ to $-0.5$). Sed$\times$0.5 removes it.
\item \textbf{At 2.5\,km and 1.25\,km the picture is consistent.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Suppression on: $S_{\rm low}$ grows with sed (0.45/0.57/0.71 at 2.5\,km; 0.53/0.57/0.70 at 1.25\,km).
\item Suppression on: $S_{\rm high}$ is near zero (0.05 at 2.5\,km; between $-0.03$ and $+0.02$ at 1.25\,km).
\item Suppression off: $S\approx0$, except sed$\times$2 at low Nd, where $S_{\rm low}\approx0.25$ because the Nd-20 deck breaks up (cloud cover 0.63--0.69).
\item $w_e$ rises with Nd and falls with the sed factor, with suppression on or off. At 1.25\,km and Nd~20 with suppression on, $w_e$ = 4.1/3.5/2.8\,mm\,s$^{-1}$ for sed $\times$0.5/$\times$1/$\times$2.
\end{itemize}
\item \textbf{LWP spatial SD} is 1--3\,g\,m$^{-2}$ at $\geq$10\,km, 6--60 at 5\,km, and 60--120 at 2.5 and 1.25\,km. It is largest at low Nd with sed$\times$2.
\item \textbf{The runs have not reached steady state.} LWP is still drifting at hour 6 in many runs (Fig.~3; e.g.\ 40\,km, Nd~20, suppression off).
Treat the hour 4--6 means as transient. The 12\,h RF02 forcing would test this.
\end{enumerate}

\item R: \texttt{source("/pscratch/sd/m/mahf708/scmlib/sed-precsupp-study/R/sedps.R")}. Interpreter: \texttt{/pscratch/sd/m/mahf708/r\_env/bin/Rscript}.
\item Code: E3SM \texttt{mahf708/eamxx/p3-cloud-sed-factor} and scmlib \texttt{sed-precsupp-study}, both on github.com/mahf708.
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Notes.} Surface precipitation includes the cloud-droplet sedimentation flux. With suppression off, autoconversion matches the default rate at Nd~63.
LWP SD is written online as a variance and its square root is taken afterwards. That is exact for the instantaneous 15-min snapshots used here.
For time-averaged streams, the mean of the variance gives the RMS SD rather than the mean SD, so a native \texttt{sqrt} would help there.
