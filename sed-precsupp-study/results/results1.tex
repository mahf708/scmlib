\section*{What the runs show}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Consistency check.} The sed$\times$1, suppression-on cells reproduce the earlier ACI-study numbers to within $\sim$1--2\,\%.
\item \textbf{At $\geq$5 km (single-column-like), the positive LWP--Nd response is all precip suppression.}
With suppression off, LWP is flat at 170--175\,g\,m$^{-2}$ ($S\approx0$), wherever $w_e$ stays near 4.9\,mm\,s$^{-1}$.
With suppression on, $S_{\rm high}\approx0.15$.
\item \textbf{Sedimentation sets an entrainment regime at coarse resolution.} At Nd 20 (sed$\times$1, $\times$2), and at Nd 50 with
sed$\times$2 at 40 km, $w_e$ drops from $\sim$4.9 to $\sim$2.5--2.9\,mm\,s$^{-1}$. This happens with suppression on or off.
With suppression off, LWP then rises to 210--230\,g\,m$^{-2}$, which gives a \emph{negative} low-Nd branch ($S_{\rm low}$ from $-0.3$ to $-0.5$).
Sed$\times$0.5 removes this regime entirely. This looks like the sedimentation--entrainment feedback, acting as a threshold in the
single-column limit.
\item \textbf{At 2.5 km}, with suppression off and sed$\times$0.5 or $\times$1, LWP is again flat ($\approx$141\,g\,m$^{-2}$), even though
$w_e$ still rises with Nd. With suppression on, $S_{\rm low}$ grows with sed (0.45, 0.57, 0.71 for $\times$0.5, $\times$1, $\times$2), and $S_{\rm high}\approx0.05$ for every sed value.
Sed$\times$2 lowers $w_e$ by 0.3--1.1\,mm\,s$^{-1}$ at every Nd. It also breaks up the low-Nd deck even with suppression off
(cloud cover 0.64--0.69 at Nd 20).
\item \textbf{LWP spatial SD} is 1--3\,g\,m$^{-2}$ at $\geq$10 km, 6--60 at 5 km and 65--120 at 2.5 km. It grows with the sed factor and is largest at low Nd.
The online value (expression \texttt{mean('col')} of the squared anomaly) matches the SD computed afterwards from the 2D LWP output.
\item \textbf{AeroCom cloud top (PR \#9017):} liquid cloud-top CDNC is $\approx$0.7$\times$ the in-cloud median Nd, consistent with SPA CCN decreasing upward.
$r_{\rm eff}$ at cloud top ranges from 8 to 21\,$\mu$m and decreases with Nd and with the sed factor.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\textbf{Notes.} Surface precipitation includes the cloud-droplet sedimentation flux.
With suppression off, the autoconversion rate matches the default at Nd 63, so rain is higher than default above that Nd and lower below it.
Susceptibility $S=d\ln{\rm LWP}/d\ln N_d$ uses the diagnosed Nd; the full table is in \texttt{susceptibility.csv}.
