Findings (hour 4-6 means, single realizations):
1. At 5 km and coarser, the positive LWP-Nd response is all precip suppression. With suppression off, LWP is flat at 170-175 g/m2.
2. At coarse resolution, sedimentation switches entrainment between two levels. At low Nd with sed x1 or x2, w_e drops to 2.5-2.9 mm/s, with suppression on or off. With suppression off this gives a negative low-Nd branch (S -0.3 to -0.5). Sed x0.5 removes it.
3. At 2.5 and 1.25 km the picture is consistent:
   - Suppression on: S_low grows with sed. It is 0.45/0.57/0.71 at 2.5 km and 0.53/0.57/0.70 at 1.25 km, for sed x0.5/x1/x2.
   - Suppression on: S_high is about zero (0.05 at 2.5 km; -0.03 to +0.02 at 1.25 km).
   - Suppression off: S is about zero, except sed x2 at low Nd (~0.25), where the Nd-20 deck breaks up (cloud cover 0.63-0.69).
   - w_e rises with Nd and falls with the sed factor, with suppression on or off. At 1.25 km and Nd 20 with suppression on, it is 4.1/3.5/2.8 mm/s for sed x0.5/x1/x2.
4. LWP spatial SD is 1-3 g/m2 at 10 km and coarser, 6-60 at 5 km, and 60-120 at 2.5 and 1.25 km.
5. The runs have not reached steady state. LWP is still drifting at hour 6 in many runs (PDF Fig. 3), so treat the hour 4-6 means as transient. The 12 h RF02 forcing would test this.

A note on LWP spatial SD without an online sqrt. The stream writes the spatial variance at instantaneous 15-min snapshots, and the square root is taken afterwards. For instantaneous output that is exact, so nothing is lost. It would matter only for time-averaged streams: the mean of the variance gives the RMS SD over the window, not the mean SD. A native sqrt would be a small addition for that case.
