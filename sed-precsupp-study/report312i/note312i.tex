\noindent{\Large\bfseries 312\,m interim results (27\,\% of the 312\,m set)}\\[2pt]
8 Nov 2026. Prepared by Claude Code (AI assistant) on behalf of Naser Mahfouz.
We have 17 of the 72 runs at 312\,m: every sed and suppression setting at Nd 20, 50 and 100, with 3D turbulence off. The exception is Nd~100, sed$\times$2, suppression off, which is still running.
All runs are 12\,h; means are over hours 10--12. Below they are compared with the same settings at 2.5\,km, 1.25\,km and 625\,m.
Nd 200--1000 and 3D turbulence on are still running, so the high-Nd branch is not covered yet.

\section*{Findings so far}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{No convergence at 312\,m. The step from 625\,m to 312\,m is the largest yet} (Fig.~\ref{fig:conv}). With sed$\times$1 and suppression on at Nd~100:
\begin{itemize}
\item LWP drops from 119 to 76\,g\,m$^{-2}$ (163 at 2.5\,km).
\item Cloud cover drops from 0.92 to 0.83.
\item $w_e$ rises from 7.0 to 8.1\,mm\,s$^{-1}$.
\item LWP spatial SD drops, and the cells shrink with $\Delta x$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:maps}).
\end{itemize}
At Nd~20 the deck breaks up into scattered small clouds: cover 0.49, LWP 39\,g\,m$^{-2}$.
\item \textbf{The low-Nd susceptibility barely changes with resolution.} $S_{20\to50}$ with suppression on is 0.56/0.73/0.97 for sed $\times$0.5/1/2 at 312\,m.
That is within $\pm$0.1 of the 2.5\,km--625\,m values (Fig.~\ref{fig:S}).
\item \textbf{The LWP peak moves to lower Nd as $\Delta x$ shrinks.} With sed$\times$1 and suppression on, $S_{50\to100}$ falls from 0.43 at 2.5\,km to 0.42, 0.29 and \textbf{0.08} at 312\,m.
So at 312\,m LWP is nearly saturated by Nd~100. Whether it falls beyond that waits on the Nd 200--1000 runs.
\item \textbf{Precip suppression matters less at 312\,m.} At Nd~100, LWP with suppression on and off is 76 vs.\ 72\,g\,m$^{-2}$, against 163 vs.\ 138 at 2.5\,km.
With suppression off, $S$ is negative on both low-Nd branches at 312\,m ($-0.11$ and $-0.13$).
\item \textbf{Steadiness.} LWP is still thinning slowly over hours 10--12 at 625\,m and 312\,m alike ($-1$ to $-4$\,g\,m$^{-2}$\,h$^{-1}$; Fig.~\ref{fig:ts}).
\end{enumerate}
