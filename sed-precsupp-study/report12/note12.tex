\section*{What changes at 12\,h}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Steadiness.} At 2.5 and 1.25\,km, $|d{\rm LWP}/dt| < 4$\,g\,m$^{-2}$\,h$^{-1}$ over hours 10--12 in every run (Fig.~\ref{fig:trend}).
These are close to equilibrium. At 40--10\,km, LWP is still rising at high Nd with suppression on (up to +18\,g\,m$^{-2}$\,h$^{-1}$; LWP reaches 340\,g\,m$^{-2}$).
\item \textbf{5\,km has joined the resolved-cell regime.} By 12\,h, LWP spatial SD is 70--140\,g\,m$^{-2}$, cloud cover drops at low Nd, and LWP and $w_e$ look like 2.5\,km.
At 6\,h, 5\,km was still single-column-like. So the transition is resolution- \emph{and} time-dependent.
\item \textbf{A falling branch at km scale.} With suppression on, $S_{200\to1000}$ is negative at both 2.5 and 1.25\,km and grows with sedimentation:
$-0.01/-0.03/-0.09$ at 1.25\,km and $-0.01/-0.03/-0.06$ at 2.5\,km for sed $\times$0.5/1/2 (Fig.~\ref{fig:S}). LWP peaks near Nd $\approx$ 200--500,
while $w_e$ keeps rising with Nd (to 6.9--7.2\,mm\,s$^{-1}$). At 6\,h this branch was barely visible.
\item \textbf{The low-Nd branch steepens with sedimentation at km scale.} With suppression on, $S_{20\to50}$ is 0.57/0.65/0.88 at 1.25\,km.
With suppression off it is $-0.02$/0.11/0.43, all of it from sedimentation-driven deck break-up (cloud cover at Nd~20 with sed$\times$2 falls below 0.6).
\item \textbf{Single-column limit (40--10\,km).} LWP with suppression on rises strongly with Nd ($S_{100\to200}$ = 0.20--0.35).
With suppression off it is flat to slightly decreasing ($S$ from $-0.01$ to $-0.1$). The sedimentation switch in $w_e$ persists at a lower level ($\approx$4.0 vs.\ $\approx$3.0\,mm\,s$^{-1}$).
\end{enumerate}
